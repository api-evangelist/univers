Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are now mainstream. They’re helping renewables perform better and businesses of all kinds manage both costs and carbon. Two things have changed: just like solar, the cost of storage has fallen dramatically, and the capabilities – especially the digital layer of software applications that operates it – have improved just as fast.
