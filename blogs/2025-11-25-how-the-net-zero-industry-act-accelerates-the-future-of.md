The European Commission’s Net-Zero Industry Act (NZIA) is Europe’s blueprint for clean-tech independence. Its mission is to turn the continent into a global leader for designing, building, and operating clean, green technologies. The transition to green power will require the EC to ramp up its manufacturing capabilities and reduce reliance on imports.
