# Univers

> Univers (formerly Envision Digital) is a Singapore-headquartered "Platform for Physical AI" company. Its EnOS platform runs real-time, autonomous operations across energy grids, buildings, transportation, and industrial manufacturing — managing 1,000+ GW of energy assets, 400M+ connected sensors and devices, for 800+ enterprise customers in 45 countries. EnOS exposes open APIs (device connection, model, asset, time-series data, alert, and data-subscription services) plus device SDKs, consumed through the EnOS Application Portal / Developer Console with access-key + secret-key request signing.
